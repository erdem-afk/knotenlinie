        How this board works

        This is a shared chat for the board. Write here as you would in any group conversation.

        Nothing becomes a research question until you confirm it, with one tap.

        Three ways to ask

        1. The agent may suggest that one of your messages reads as a testable question. Tap “Yes, record it”, or “No, just chatting”.
        2. Choose “Forward to agent” on any message, yours or a colleague's, and add a note if you like.
        3. Write @knotenlinie in a message.

        What happens next

        The agent either answers from past work, with references to the ledger, or drafts a test plan. Nothing runs until you approve the plan.

        Before a test runs, its plan is frozen. Every result goes into the open ledger, including “no effect”.

        What this work can and cannot do

        It tests specific, clearly stated questions against data. It does not give absolute earthquake probabilities, for Istanbul or anywhere else, and we never call it “prediction”.

        What is kept

        Everything written here is stored. You can hide your own message; that erases its text for everyone, including us. Your name appears in the public ledger only if you choose so, and you can change that later from the menu under your name.
